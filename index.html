Not Sure if Your Tree is Dangerous?

Don't guess. If you've got a tree leaning toward your roof, cracked branches over your driveway, roots lifting your paving, or overgrowth near your fence or power lines — send us photos or request a site visit. We'll give you a straight answer.

Common Risk Signs to Watch:

  • 🌳 Tree leaning toward roof or structure
  • 🌳 Cracked or hanging branches
  • 🌳 Dead branches above walkways or vehicles
  • 🌳 Roots lifting driveway or walls
  • 🌳 Tree touching power lines
  • 🌳 Hollow trunk or decay visible
  • 🌳 Tree damaged by previous storms
